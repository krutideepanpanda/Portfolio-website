// PART 01: TECHNICAL & INDUSTRY PROFILE
Professional Architecture & Expertise
Silicon Design Engineer 2 at AMD • Former Intern at Intel & Texas Instruments
AMS Modeling • Formal Signoff • CAD Automation
I am a Silicon Design Engineer 2 at AMD specializing as an Analog Mixed-Signal (AMS) Design Engineer with a primary focus on behavioral modeling. In this core role, I perform rigorous formal verification and logic equivalence checking using industry-standard Cadence Conformal LEC and Synopsys ESP, ensuring seamless signoff across complex mixed-signal interfaces.
The XMODEL Game-Changer for AMS Simulation
A key pillar of my technical capability is behavioral modeling and AMS testing using XMODEL and MODELZEN by Scientific Analog. Traditional SPICE simulation for mixed-signal SoCs creates massive bottlenecks, often taking days for regression runs. XMODEL is a true game-changer: by introducing event-driven Real-Number Modeling (RNM) directly into SystemVerilog environments, it simulates analog circuits at digital simulation speeds (up to 1,000x faster than SPICE) while preserving exact waveform accuracy and functional continuity. This empowers rapid architectural exploration and comprehensive top-level regression verification before silicon tapeout.
Beyond my core AMS modeling duties, my passion for custom scripting and software engineering enabled me to take initiative in architecting complete internal CAD automations and regression test suites at AMD. By leveraging Python and TCL scripting, I significantly improve verification productivity and deliver production-quality internal CAD tooling and regression automation.
Prior to joining AMD full-time, I built foundational industry experience during undergraduate engineering internships at Intel Corporation (performing structural IR drop reliability checks with Ansys Redhawk) and Texas Instruments (engineering high-speed DAC calibration and trimming algorithms).
