Professional profile

I am a Silicon Design Engineer 2 at AMD specializing in analog mixed-signal behavioral modeling, formal verification, and CAD automation.

My core work uses XMODEL and MODELZEN to create fast real-number modeling environments, alongside Cadence Conformal LEC and Synopsys ESP for rigorous signoff. Around that foundation, I build Python and TCL automation that turns repetitive engineering flows into dependable systems.
