VLSI • AI CAD Automation • Analog Modeling

Experienced Silicon Design Engineer with 3+ years of industry expertise across top semiconductor leaders. At AMD, I specialize in developing mission-critical internal CAD automation frameworks using Claude Code, autonomous AI agents, custom agent skills, and automation hooks to accelerate analog circuit sizing and formal verification cycles.

My technical arsenal spans system-level analog and mixed-signal modeling using XMODEL and MODELZEN by Scientific Analog, alongside rigorous formal verification and logic equivalence checking using industry-standard Synopsys ESP and Cadence Conformal LEC. Prior to AMD, I executed structural IR drop reliability checks at Intel Corporation and engineered high-speed DAC calibration algorithms at Texas Instruments during undergraduate internships.
